# G5 — Used 5+ years, private seller, two months' wait with a rental

2 months with an 8 000 kr/month rental meanwhile (delay cost 16 000 kr at
month 0). 100 % odds at 12. The schedule's month 1 starts at delivery,
2027-03-01, so the 12 taxed months are March 2027 – February 2028 (leap year):
366 days × 10 kr = 3 660 kr.

Cash up front = 150 000 + 10 000 + 5 000 + 16 000 = **181 000 kr**.

Cash to 12: 181 000 + 12 000 + 3 660 + 2 400 + 1 200 = 200 260. Exit terms:
−135 000 + 6 750 = −128 250. TCO(12) = **72 010 kr**.

Display at 12: delay 16 000; fees and taxes = 5 000 + 3 660 = 8 660.
