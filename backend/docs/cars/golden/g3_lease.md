# G3 — 36-month lease: early exit at 12, term end at 36, repeat lease to 48

Inputs: 5 000 kr/month, 30 000 kr upfront rent, 2 000 kr fees, 10 000 km/yr
cap, 2 kr per excess km, early exit = 50 % of the remaining payments, 3 000 kr
end-of-lease fee, nothing included. Odds 25/0/50/25.

Cash up front = 2 000 + 30 000 + 10 000 (tyres) = **42 000 kr**.

Exit at 12: cash = 2 000 + 30 000 + 12 × 5 000 + 10 000 + 12 000 + 3 650 +
2 400 + 1 200 = 121 250. Exit terms: early exit 50 % × 24 × 5 000 = 60 000;
excess km: 12 months into the term allow 10 000 km, driven 12 000 → 2 000 km
× 2 kr = 4 000; end fee 3 000 → 67 000. TCO(12) = **188 250 kr**.

Exit at 36 (term end): cash = 2 000 + 30 000 + 180 000 + 10 000 + 36 000 +
10 960 + 7 200 + 3 600 = 279 760. Exit terms: early exit 0; excess km 36 000 −
30 000 = 6 000 km × 2 = 12 000; end fee 3 000 → 15 000. TCO(36) = **294 760 kr**.

Exit at 48 (a repeat lease starts in month 37, flagged Estimate): cash =
279 760 + 30 000 (new upfront) + 12 × 5 000 + 12 000 + 3 650 (2030) + 2 400 +
1 200 = 389 010. Exit terms: 12 months into the second term → early exit
60 000, excess km 4 000, end fee 3 000 → 67 000. TCO(48) = **456 010 kr**.
