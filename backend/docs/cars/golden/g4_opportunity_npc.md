# G4 — Opportunity cost and discounting in isolation

Inputs: 100 000 kr paid at month 0, resale flat at 100 000 kr, no friction,
no insurance, energy, tyres, service or taxes (zero-rule fixture); return on
cash 12 %/yr. Odds 50/50 at 12 and 24 months.

Growth factors g_m = 1,12^(m/12): g_12 = 1,12, g_24 = 1,2544.

Opportunity cost C_opp(t) = 100 000 × (g_t − 1): **12 000 kr** at 12,
**25 440 kr** at 24. TCO(t) = 100 000 − 100 000 + C_opp(t) = C_opp(t).

Net present cost NPC(t) = 100 000 − 100 000 / g_t:
at 12: 100 000 − 89 285,71 = **10 714,29 kr**;
at 24: 100 000 − 79 719,39 = **20 280,61 kr**.
